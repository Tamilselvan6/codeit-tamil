Water Jug Puzzle Solver

Puzzle: You have three jugs with capacities of 8 liters, 5 liters, and 3 liters. The 8-liter jug is full of water, while the other two are empty. Your task is to divide the water into two equal parts (4 liters in one jug and 4 liters in another) using only these three jugs.

How to play: Click on a jug to select it (it will highlight), then click on another jug to pour water from the first selected jug to the second one.

Keyboard Shortcuts:

1 Select Jug A
2 Select Jug B
3 Select Jug C
